02 / OUR INITIATIVE
✳　 WHY CLASS EXISTS

Because a diagram
is not the same as
discovery.

Too many students meet science through a page before they ever get to meet it through an experiment.

We are building CLASS — The Student Lab to make scientific ideas feel tangible. A place to test, observe, compare, make mistakes safely, and understand the why behind the answer. Access to a laboratory should not be the boundary of a student's curiosity.
